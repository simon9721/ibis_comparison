## The rule

An entry **stays** in `results/` if any of these holds:

- a script written or edited since 09-08 (the gate-state / track 1 / track 2 period: 70 scripts,
  83 with their imports, plus `scripts/deck_0918/`) names it - as a path it reads, or as a
  source cited in deck speaker notes;
- it was produced since 09-08;
- it is a cache (`_golden_hspice_cache`), or the run log of a study that stays.

Everything else was moved. The date cut is 09-08 because that is where the gate-state
model and the two tracks start; the command-layer / stress-pedestal work of 09-01 to 09-07 is
recorded in its FINDINGS and in memory, and no current script reads it.

Every folder in `results/` and `results/archive/` now has a markdown write-up. 122 of them are
generated READMEs (contents, producing script and its docstring, first log lines), marked as
such in their first lines: 38 in folders already archived (a 39th, for a folder that was
already empty, is a two-line note), 54 in the entries below, 30 in folders that stay.
A generated README says what a folder is and what made it; it is not a
study write-up.
