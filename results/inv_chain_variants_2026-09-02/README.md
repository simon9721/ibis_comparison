# inv_chain variants

Generated by `scripts/make_inv_chain_variants.py`. Each variant changes one property of the silicon and inherits everything else from the shipped `inv_chain` recipe.

| variant | stages | final m | Wn (um) | Wp (um) | what changed |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| `base8` | 8 | 128 | 1 | 2 | eight stages, x2 taper (reference build) |
| `stage4` | 4 | 128 | 1 | 2 | four stages, same final drive |
| `skewp` | 8 | 128 | 1 | 1 | weak PMOS, Wp = Wn |
| `weak` | 8 | 128 | 0.5 | 1 | half drive at every stage |

**`base8`** — Identical to the shipped inv_chain. Built here so every variant, including the reference, comes out of the same generator.

**`stage4`** — Halves the predriver depth while leaving the output stage alone, so the fitted onset delays should shorten and nothing else should.

**`skewp`** — Makes rise slower than fall on purpose, to see whether the asymmetry that produces io_buf's dead zone reproduces on a device where we set its size.

**`weak`** — Slower edges into the same load, with the timing structure unchanged.
