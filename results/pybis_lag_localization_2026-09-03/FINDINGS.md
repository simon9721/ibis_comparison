# The pybis lag and the ~3x gap are one thing: an output-stage delay

Returning to the two pybis artifacts seen across five silicons -- pybis runs
7-9 ps later than native IBIS and sits at ~3x its pad RMSE against the
transistor. Both read the same IBIS file, so the difference is in how each turns
the tables into a waveform. This localizes it.

Probed on inv_chain base8, full swing, Ku and pad measured on both builds
(native exposes Ku via `xv_pu=ku`, pybis as an internal node).

## The lag is in the output stage, not the command layer

Rising-edge 50% crossings:

| signal | native IBIS | pybis | pybis − native |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Ku | 5.2807 ns | 5.2825 ns | **+1.7 ps** |
| pad | 5.3320 ns | 5.3419 ns | **+9.9 ps** |

pybis's input comparator fires at +0.8 ps and its Ku edge lands within 1.7 ps of
native's. **The command layer -- edge detection, the 1.4 V comparator, the 10 ps
transport line -- is not the cause.** The ~8 ps enters between Ku and the pad:
the output stage, where Ku/Kd scale the I-V currents into C_comp and the load.

Package parasitics are ruled out -- R_pkg, L_pkg, C_pkg are all zero in the
generated subcircuit, so the pad topology is C_comp (0.468 pF) plus the load,
the same as native IBIS's bench.

## The lag *is* the gap

pybis vs native IBIS, pad RMSE over the rising edge:

| | RMSE |
|---|---:|
| as-is | 17.34 mV |
| pybis shifted −9.0 ps | 2.26 mV |

**87% of the pybis-vs-native difference is a pure time shift.** Remove the ~9 ps
and the two pads agree to 2.3 mV. So the "7-9 ps lag" and the "~3x RMSE gap" are
not two problems -- they are the same output-stage delay, seen once in the time
domain and once in the error norm. A 9 ps shift on an 18 ps edge is a large
RMSE; that is where the 3x comes from.

This is the useful consequence: there is one thing to fix, not two, and it lives
in one place -- the conversion from Ku(t) to pad voltage.

## A secondary artifact, filtered out here

pybis's Ku rings through the transition (overshoot to 1.05, oscillation down to
0.2) where native's Ku is smooth. On this bench C_comp and the load filter it,
so it barely reaches the pad -- it is not what drives the RMSE gap. But it is
real, and on a lighter load or a coefficient-level comparison it would matter.

## What is not yet answered

*Why* the output stage adds ~9 ps. The leading hypothesis: the V-T waveform
native IBIS replays was recorded at the pad with C_comp already present, while
pybis reconstructs the pad by driving the I-V currents through an explicit
C_comp -- so C_comp's effect may be applied twice, adding an RC delay. Testing
that (e.g. re-running pybis with C_comp reduced and checking whether the lag
tracks) is the next step and would confirm the mechanism before any fix.
